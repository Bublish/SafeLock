# SafeLock - Password Generator — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: May 14, 2026**

SafeLock - Password Generator ("the Extension") is designed with privacy as a core principle.

## Data we collect

None. The Extension does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

## How passwords are generated

All passwords are generated locally on your device using the browser's built-in `crypto.getRandomValues` API. No generated password is ever sent to any server.

## Settings storage

Your preferences (password length and character set options) are stored locally using Chrome's built-in `chrome.storage.sync`. This syncs across your own Chrome devices via your Google account — it never reaches our servers.

## Clipboard

After copying a password, the Extension makes a best-effort attempt to clear your clipboard after 10 seconds. This is entirely local.

## Third parties

The Extension does not use analytics, ads, or any third-party services.
